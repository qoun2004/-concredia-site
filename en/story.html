Our Story · The Lonely Highway

Inside every grain of concrete,
there is a stretch of lonely highway.

Carrying a 70kg concrete table, driving four and a half hours to Xúhǎi. Where the mountains meet the sea and the signal fades — letting concrete meet people.

Concrete Village

One village. One PhD.
Six years of solitary practice.

In the 1960s and 70s, a construction boom swept Taiwan. The men of Tiantouli, Dounan Township, Yunlin County poured out to work on nearby construction sites as concrete laborers. The village earned a nickname: "Concrete Village" (孔固力庄).

At 47, Rovi Lee made a decision: to reclaim his identity as a "Concrete Village boy" and use cement as his medium for creation. The year was 2016.

2020 · Island Circuit on Foot

52 days · 1,420 km
What has become of Taiwan?

In 2020–2021, Rovi walked the entire island of Taiwan on foot. Mountains, coasts, industrial zones, farmland — everywhere he looked, he saw mountains of construction waste with nowhere to go.

The EAC process, the recycled aggregate formulas, the complete SOP — none of this was accidental. It was the systematic answer accumulated over five years following that walk.

Brand Origin

2,190 Days in
the Warehouse

From 2016 to 2022, Rovi spent six years alone in the warehouse — mixing, casting, curing, grinding, failing, starting over. Every formula adjustment, every formwork iteration, every grinding sequence is a chapter in a research log that only exists in his hands.

The EAC (Exposed Aggregate Concrete) process was born here. A method that lets the true identity of the waste material surface — its geological memory made visible.

The Lonely Highway Project

Travel with Concredia goods.

Taiwan Provincial Highway 26 — the missing link in Taiwan's island circuit road, stopped by ecological conservation. The stretch from Xúhǎi to Nántián: just 9 kilometres as the crow flies, yet 40 minutes by car on winding mountain roads.

This is Rovi's "Lonely Highway". The plan: carry the Stand-bar high table into Taiwan's mountain and coastal landscapes. Where the cold hardness of concrete meets the vast expanse of sea and sky — that extreme contrast is our most honest brand language.

Structure · Reinforcement

Bridges have rebar. We build a table — and we add rebar too. Not because we must, but because that is the correct way. The instinct of a civil engineer cannot be switched off.

Form
Formwork · PS Board

Every piece requires a mold. Design the structure, cut the formwork, pour, wait, strip, grind. No shortcuts, no substitutes. PS board — heat-cut or blade-cut to any shape — is the proprietary core of the process.

Cure
Waiting · 28 Days

Concrete needs time. 24 hours to initial set, 28 days to reach design strength. We don't rush. We wait. Good things are worth waiting for.

Grind
Finishing · EAC Reveal

EAC aggregate exposure is achieved through grinding — pass by pass by pass. Grinding out the original colour of the waste material, grinding out the geological memory, grinding out the unique face of each piece.

Raw Materials

Every bag of sand has a provenance.

What determines the texture of a piece is not how much water was added — it is where the aggregate came from. Every batch of sand, slag powder, and waste glass is personally traced by Rovi, its recycled ratio confirmed, before it enters the formula.

On-site Materials · 70%
Recycle Aggregates

Waste aggregate, waste brick, waste glass, slag powder. These form the body of each work — collected from demolition sites and industrial waste streams across Taiwan.

Off-site Materials · 30%
Virgin Aggregates + Cement

Virgin sand and cement remain the irreplaceable structural foundation. Rovi sources them directly from trusted suppliers to ensure consistent quality.

Structural Materials
Steel · Rebar + Wire

12-gauge formwork wire, hand-bent piece by piece into the required form. Each artwork requires 2+ hours. Rebar suspended at exactly 1cm centre — ensuring tensile integrity throughout.

Formwork Process · PS Board

Why not wood formwork?

When wood formwork bonds with concrete during the shrinkage phase, the wood's pores grip the surface. Stripping the form often requires sawing out the entire plank — labour-intensive, imprecise. Rovi instead uses PS board (polystyrene), which can be precision-cut by heat or blade into any shape, is lightweight, strips cleanly, and is the proprietary core of the EAC process.

"The cut is the quietest moment. One pass of the blade decides the boundary of this piece forever."

Formula Confidentiality

Not patented. Not published. Irreproducible.

Just as a ceramicist keeps their glaze ratios private, Concredia.Lab's aggregate formula is the core asset of years of R&D. Filing a patent means publishing it. Publishing it means competitors can adjust by one variable and work around it. We protect this knowledge through SOP licensing — not through public disclosure.

"Coca-Cola's formula was never patented. And no one has ever replicated it exactly."

Brand Timeline
