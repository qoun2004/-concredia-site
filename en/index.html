Craftsman · Artisan Spirit

The gentlest hands in Taiwan,
crafting the most enduring works.

A PhD in civil engineering, making furniture. The precision of bridge construction, applied to a table. Steel reinforcement, formwork, curing, grinding — every step as rigorous as a building.

Waste is not worthless — it is a resource misplaced. We transform Taiwan's construction debris into furniture, speakers, and art installations.
